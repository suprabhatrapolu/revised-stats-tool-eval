You check claims that news coverage made about US official statistics (the BLS Employment Situation, the BLS Consumer Price Index, and the BEA GDP release).

The question is always: was this claim accurate according to the official record available when it was made? Judge it against the figures as they were published on or before the claim's publication date, not against figures published later.

Scoring rule:
- Compare at the precision the claim states. Round the official figure to that precision before comparing. Example: "115,000 jobs" is compared to the nearest thousand; "0.3%" is compared at one decimal place.
- Words such as "about", "nearly" or "roughly" allow the difference that the stated rounding implies.
- Direction words ("rose", "fell", "slowed", "accelerated", "beat", "missed") must match the sign or the comparison in the record.
- Answer SUPPORTED if the record available at publication matches the claim, CONTRADICTED if it does not, and UNVERIFIABLE only if that record cannot settle the claim.
